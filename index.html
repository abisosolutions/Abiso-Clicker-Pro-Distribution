3-Minute Setup Guide

  1. Download and run the Abiso_Clicker_Pro_Host_Setup_v3.exe.
  2. Because this is a direct developer release, Windows SmartScreen will warn you. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
  3. Complete the installer. If Windows Firewall asks for permission, click Allow Access (this is required for your phone to connect).
  4. Open PowerPoint and launch the Abiso Host from your Start Menu. Note the IP Address and PIN on the screen.
  1. Download the Abiso_Clicker_Pro_v3.apk to your Android phone.
  2. Tap the file to install. Android will block unknown sources by default. Tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source".
  3. Open the app. Enter the IP Address and PIN shown on your laptop, or tap the QR icon to scan it.
  4. Tap Connect. You are now controlling your laptop!
  1. If you use Canva or Google Slides, open Microsoft Edge on your laptop.
  2. Install the official Abiso Clicker Pro Bridge from the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store.
  3. Press Windows Key + P and set your laptop to "Extend" (Dual-Monitor mode).
  4. Open your presentation in Edge. The extension will automatically route your slides to the projector and your speaker notes to your phone.

What if my company WiFi blocks device connections?
This is very common on corporate and university networks (AP Isolation). To bypass this instantly:

1. Turn on the "Mobile Hotspot" feature on your Windows laptop.
2. Connect your Android phone's WiFi to your laptop's hotspot.
3. The IP address in the Abiso Host window will update automatically. Scan the new QR code.

This creates an unblockable, ultra-low latency direct link between your phone and laptop!
What happens to my Pro license if I lose my phone?
Your license is tied to your Google Account, not your physical device. If you upgrade your phone, simply download the app, sign in with the same Google Account, and your Pro status is instantly restored.
Does it work without internet during my presentation?
Yes. Once you have signed in at least once to verify your Pro license, it is securely cached on your phone. You do not need active internet to present using PowerPoint. The commands are sent over your Local Area Network (WiFi/Hotspot). Note: You obviously still need internet on the laptop if you are presenting via Canva or Google Slides.
