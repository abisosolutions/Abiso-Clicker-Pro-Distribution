3-Minute Setup Guide

  1. Choose your installation method:
    • Option A (Recommended All-in-One): Download and run Abiso_Clicker_Pro_Host_Setup_v3.exe. This installer automatically bundles and configures the PowerPoint Media Bridge.
    • Option B (Microsoft Store): Install via the Microsoft Store for sandboxed installation.
  2. If prompted by Windows SmartScreen on the direct installer, click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
  3. Allow firewall permissions when prompted (this allows your phone to connect over Wi-Fi).
  4. Launch the Abiso Host. Note the IP Address and PIN shown on your screen.
  1. Download the Abiso_Clicker_Pro_v3.apk to your Android phone.
  2. Tap the file to install. If prompted by Android, tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source".
  3. Open the app. Enter the IP Address and PIN shown on your laptop, or tap the QR icon to scan the screen.
  4. Tap Connect. You are now controlling your laptop!
  1. If you present using Canva or Google Slides, open Microsoft Edge on your laptop.
  2. Install the official Abiso Clicker Pro Bridge from the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store.
  3. Press Windows Key + P and set your display to "Extend" (Dual-Monitor mode).
  4. Open your presentation in Edge. The extension will automatically route your slides to the projector and your speaker notes to your phone.
  1. Do you need this?
    • If you downloaded the All-in-One .exe (Card 1), NO—it was already installed automatically.
    • If you installed the Host from the Microsoft Store, YES—download Abiso-PowerPoint-Bridge-Setup.exe.
  2. Run Abiso-PowerPoint-Bridge-Setup.exe on your laptop.
  3. The installer registers the lightweight VSTO Add-in with PowerPoint.
  4. Now you can play and pause slide videos directly from your phone's Media Drawer, even when PowerPoint is running on an extended display or in the background!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download the PowerPoint Media Bridge Add-in?
• If you downloaded the All-in-One .exe from this website: NO. The PowerPoint Media Bridge is already bundled into the installer and configured automatically.

• If you installed Abiso from the Microsoft Store: YES. Because Microsoft Store packages are sandboxed by Windows security policies, the store package cannot auto-register Office COM add-ins. Simply download and run Abiso-PowerPoint-Bridge-Setup.exe once to bridge PowerPoint video playback.
Why is Abiso available as both a Direct Download and on the Microsoft Store?
We provide both options for maximum convenience:

• Direct All-in-One Installer (.exe): Best for general users because it bundles the Windows Host and PowerPoint Media Bridge into a single, seamless 1-click setup.
• Microsoft Store Listing: Ideal for corporate or locked-down IT laptops where users are restricted from running external installers and must install software through the official Windows Store.

What if my company WiFi blocks device connections?
This is very common on corporate and university networks (AP Isolation). To bypass this instantly:

1. Turn on the "Mobile Hotspot" feature on your Windows laptop.
2. Connect your Android phone's WiFi to your laptop's hotspot.
3. The IP address in the Abiso Host window will update automatically. Scan the new QR code.

This creates an unblockable, ultra-low latency direct link between your phone and laptop!
What happens to my Pro license if I lose my phone?
Your license is tied to your Google Account, not your physical device. If you upgrade your phone, simply download the app, sign in with the same Google Account, and your Pro status is instantly restored.
Does it work without internet during my presentation?
Yes. Once you have signed in at least once to verify your Pro license, it is securely cached on your phone. You do not need active internet to present using PowerPoint. The commands are sent over your Local Area Network (WiFi/Hotspot). Note: You obviously still need internet on the laptop if you are presenting via Canva or Google Slides.
